# Looprun closed test: tester guide

Thanks for helping. You will install an early version of Looprun on your Android phone and run with it a few times over two weeks. Staying opted in for the whole period matters: it is a Google requirement for the app to be released.

## 1. Join the test

1. You need an **Android phone (Android 7 or newer)** and a Google account (the one you use in the Play Store).
2. Send the organiser the Google account email you will use. They add it to the tester list.
3. Open the opt-in link: [OPT_IN_LINK]. Sign in with the same account and tap **Become a tester**.
4. Tap the Play Store link on that page and **Install**. It can take a few hours to appear.
5. **Do not opt out or uninstall between [TEST_START] and [TEST_END].** Leaving early removes you from the count.

## 2. What to try (about 15 minutes, plus real runs)

1. Open Looprun and sign in with Google.
2. Read the location screen, tap **Agree and continue**, allow location "while using the app" and notifications.
3. Open the map (lane **1 MAP**): it should centre on you (if not, tell us). Try the four colour themes in lane **3 YOU**.
4. Tap **START RUN** on the map. Run or walk a loop outdoors of **at least 600 metres in length (roughly 1 km is safer), enclosing a real block or park**, ending near where you started. Very small loops are rejected as too small.
5. Lock the phone halfway. The Looprun notification should stay. Unlock: progress should still show. Do **not** swipe Looprun away from the recent-apps list during a run: that ends the recording (the next start of the app offers to finish and upload what was recorded).
6. Stop the run, either with the Stop button in the notification or with **FINISH RUN** in the app. Check the result screen: distance, time, cells claimed.
7. Open the map and find your cells. Open the board (lane **2 BOARD**). The map should update by itself when another tester takes a cell (live updates; others' changes appear after about 30 minutes, by design).
8. Try **Report a cell** on the map and **Report a problem with this run** on a result screen. The reason is free text and is stored for 180 days: do not type personal details.
9. Lane 3 YOU: add a home zone, start rest days, then end them.
10. Run again another day over some of the same streets.
11. Social pass (needs two testers with two accounts, called A and B here):
    1. Rename yourself in the app. Try a name that should be rejected: you should see a generic message that does not say why.
    2. A searches for B (at least 2 characters of the name) and follows B.
    3. B finishes a run. It should appear in A's Feed only about 30 minutes after it was processed.
    4. A gives kudos, writes a comment, then deletes the comment.
    5. After 30 minutes B's Inbox shows the new follower, the kudos and the comment.
    6. A takes a cell from B. After 30 minutes check Battles (summary, rivals) and "take back" on B's side.
    7. Report a run card, a comment and a profile (do not type personal details).
    8. A blocks B. Feed, comments, search and profile should be hidden both ways, while the map still shows B as owner of cells.
    9. A unblocks B.
12. Optional, on a throwaway account: Profile > Delete account, and confirm you are signed out.

Runs upload while Looprun is open; a run finished without a connection waits on the phone and goes up the next time the app is open with a connection.

Please do not use fake GPS apps unless the organiser asks you to, and run safely.

**Not in this build, so please do not test or report as missing:** crash reporting and the web account-deletion page (deleting works inside the app only for now), push notifications (the Inbox is read inside the app), profile photos, and a card view for a run opened from a notification (it shows the comments only).

## 3. Report bugs

Send to [CONTACT_EMAIL] or [FEEDBACK_CHANNEL]. Include phone brand and model, Android version, what you did / expected / saw, a screenshot or screen recording if you can, the approximate time, and whether it happens every time. Do not send your exact home address.

## 4. Which phones we want

Variety matters more than flagships: Samsung, Xiaomi / Redmi / POCO, Google Pixel, OnePlus, Oppo / Realme, Motorola, and any Android 7+ phone. These brands are known for aggressive battery saving, which can stop GPS with the screen off: if recording stops, tell us the brand and whether you changed battery settings. Android 13 and newer are especially useful.

## 5. Privacy during the test

Precise location during a run, a one-off position to centre the map and to place home zones, the home-zone centre, and your Google account ID and first name are used. The map picture comes from [MAP_TILE_PROVIDER]. Raw GPS of your finished runs also stays on your phone until you delete your account or uninstall. Free-text reports you send are stored for 180 days. Follows, kudos, comments and blocks are stored until you delete your account; battle history for 90 days and notifications for 60 days. Your display name can be changed in the app and other players can see it. Delete your account in the app at any time; policy: [PRIVACY_POLICY_URL].
