## Full description

Run a loop. Fill the map.

Looprun turns your city into a shared game board of hexagon cells. Head out for a run, close a loop, and the cells inside it can become yours. Other runners can take cells from you, but only by putting in real running time.

HOW IT WORKS
- Tap Start run and run a route that loops back to where you began.
- When you finish, the server works out your loops and the cells inside them.
- Your power in a cell grows each time you run there and slowly fades if you stay away, so steady runners beat one fast run.
- The weekly leaderboard shows who holds the most cells.
- On the map, you see your cells and, where enough runners are nearby, the names of other owners.

BUILT FOR FAIR PLAY
- The phone records, the server decides. Runs that look like driving, teleporting or fake GPS are held back for review.

YOUR PRIVACY
- Precise location is recorded while you run, with a notification always visible. The app also reads your position once to centre the map and to place a home zone.
- Hide your home: set up to five home zones where your name never appears.
- Other players see which cells you own, never your route. Changes of owner show up for others after a delay.
- No ads. We do not sell your data.
- Delete your account in the app at any time. Your runs and settings are removed, and your cells pass to the strongest remaining player or become free.

FEATURES
- Map of claimed cells
- Weekly leaderboard
- Pause your decay with a freeze when you are away (up to 30 days)
- Sign in with Google

Looprun is for adults aged 18 and over. Run safely, follow local rules, and stay aware of traffic and other people. Looprun is a game, not a medical or training tool.
